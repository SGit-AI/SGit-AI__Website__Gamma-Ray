# Coverage

GAMMA-Ray uses a share of each instrument, and says which. A provision counts at the lowest tier at which one of its indicators can be answered.

{{gen:coverage-table}}

## What it says

- The instruments that rate **the agent** (the protocol's authorisation rules, EAAMM's levels, OWASP's tiers, Salesforce's levels) are the ones that can be read from outside.
- The instruments that rate **the organisation** (Microsoft, NIST, most of AAGMM) need the operator's word or evidence. That is what the Declared and Evidenced tiers add.
- These shares are of the provisions as we cut them. Cutting an instrument differently changes the numbers.

Earlier counts, made by hand before the mapping existed, are in the [research notes](../research/outside-coverage-first-pass.md); the figures on this page are computed from the data and replace them.
