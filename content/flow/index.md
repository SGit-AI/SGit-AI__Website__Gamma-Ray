Trust rises in two ways: more people and agents stand behind a fact (Accountability), and the source moves closer to the company and its customers (Source). Our own reading of a public page, however careful, stays at Source 1. The strongest claim is the company's own, on its own domain, checked by someone who answers for it.

## Two loops

- A review finding goes back to the mapper, who answers it in the scan before the next round.
- A correction from the company or a customer goes back to the scorer. Nothing is deleted: the old fact is superseded, and every page that depended on it changes with it.

## Where it stands

{{gen:flow-intake}}. A company appears on this site only when its card has passed two rounds of review and a sign-off; until then it is in the repository's `data/intake/`, and no photo that shows people is kept there.

The flow is data: [flow.json](../data/flow.json). The newsroom of [PrompTugal.news](https://promptugal.news/) reads it to draw the same picture for its own pieces.
