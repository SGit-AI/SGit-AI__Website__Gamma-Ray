# The instruments

Standards, frameworks, a protocol specification, a law and one published model. They are different kinds of thing: a law binds, a framework is adopted by choice, a vendor's article is neither. Each is kept whole and cited; none is merged into another.

{{gen:instruments}}

## Read, and not yet in the graph

- **AgentStripes** (arXiv 2610.18603): an evidence-graded maturity model for one released agent, from its repository; 40 capabilities in 12 areas. Its rule that the evidence level "is not chosen by the judges" is the model for ours.
- **SEI and Accenture, AI Adoption Maturity Model** (8 June 2026): eight dimensions, five levels, about AI adoption in general.
- **Cloud Security Alliance, Agentic AI Governance Maturity Model**: a draft of 27 March 2026, with a 22-question self-assessment.
- **Feng, McDonald and Zhang, Levels of Autonomy for AI Agents** (arXiv 2506.12469): the source of the five user roles.
