# GAMMA-Ray in two minutes

<p class="lede">A draft standard that describes an AI agent from public evidence. It is a beta, tried out at Web Summit Lisbon, and published for review.</p>

## What it is

GAMMA-Ray looks at one AI agent and writes down two things: how much it does on its own, and how well that is controlled. Every statement comes from a public source, and the source is linked.

The name stands for Graph-Analyzed Multi-framework Maturity Assessment with Risk-Adjusted Yield. It is Dinis Cruz's name.

## Where it comes from

The brief began with five published maturity models for agents. Read at source, they were not all what they were said to be, and some rate the company, not the agent. [The corrections to the brief](../about/brief-corrections.md) say what changed.

The graph now holds **{{n:standards}} instruments**, cut into **{{n:provisions}} provisions**. We found the observable fact behind each and grouped the facts into **{{n:questions}} questions**. One answer counts towards several instruments at once. [The instruments](../instruments/index.md) lists each with its source, licence and the date it was read.

The mapping between instruments is a first pass by one reader. A second reader has not checked it.

## How to read a card

{{gen:spectrum-cards}}

A card has four dimensions: Autonomy, Reach, Control and Openness. Each has four levels. The signature, for example A3 · R3 · C2 · O3, leads, so a product is described by its shape and not by one number.

- A level is computed from a published rule. No person assigns it.
- A card says what is public. What is missing appears only as the next unlock: the one public page that would move a level.
- "Not yet evidenced" is never "no".
- Marketing, roadmaps and demonstrations are shown and never counted.
- Each level also shows the strongest band of the facts behind it, so you can see how well a level is backed. Every card shows its status. [FACT-Ray](../fact-ray/index.md) is the provisional scheme for those bands.

## What is rated so far

{{n:cards}} cards: our own {{n:deployments}} agents, and {{n:products}} products, each read from its vendor's public documentation. A product is rated per configuration. None of the vendors has been shown its card. Some have passed two rounds of review and a sign-off and the rest are still in review; [the pipeline page](../spectrum/pipeline/index.md) shows where each one stands.

Behind the card is the governance rating, for whoever operates an agent. It uses RiskMandate's lens: what an agent is allowed to do (its grant) is measured beside the questions, counted and shown, never scored. [The rating](../rating/index.md) · [the lens](../lens/index.md)

## How far to trust a fact

A level says what a card claims. [FACT-Ray](../fact-ray/index.md) says how far to trust each fact behind it: who says it, how firmly it is recorded, and who stands behind it. It is trust, not truth. A score is always written with its three coordinates, for example S1·R4·A0, and never as "verified".

Today every card is a Draft: we found the facts, captured and hashed the sources, and other agents checked them. A company can answer for itself by publishing one file at `/.well-known/fact-ray.json` on its own domain. That is the strongest thing it can do. No company has published one yet. This site publishes its own.

## What is beta

- Everything. Every page carries the banner and every rating a beta label.
- The name Spectrum is a working name.
- The rules are written, run on real evidence, and still moving. [What they did](../spectrum/findings/index.md).
- Every card is a Draft: found and checked by agents of one model family in one repository. No human has signed one, no company has confirmed one, and no customer has attested one.
- Review by other agents is running. Most cards were mapped by one agent and have not finished it. [The workflow](../spectrum/pipeline/index.md).
- The links between indicators and provisions have not been checked one by one.

## Public evidence, public rating

A rating made only from public sources may be published, in the way public ratings of websites and companies are. For anyone but us it needs a basis, a grade of documented or better on every answer, a statement that its subject has not seen it, and a dated correction when a fact is shown to be wrong.

## If you are the company

If a fact on your card is wrong, or a public page exists that we did not find, tell us on [the corrections page](../feedback/index.md). A link is the fastest way to move a level. A correction is made in place and dated. You can also ask to claim the card there.

The evidence for each scan is in a public vault, and its read key is on the scan's page. [All scans](../scans/index.md).
