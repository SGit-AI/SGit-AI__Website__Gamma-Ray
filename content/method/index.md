# How a card is made

**Source.** Only public pages. Each source is linked on the card, captured once, and kept in the scan's public vault with its SHA-256.

**Evidence.** Each answer is one observable fact, with a source, a grade and a mode: deployed, default or available. A claim of "available" is never shown as "deployed".

**Level.** A published rule turns the answers into four levels (Autonomy, Reach, Control, Openness). The rules are in [the evidence map](../spectrum/evidence-map/index.md). A person or an agent maps evidence to facts. Neither assigns a level.

**Standards.** The facts are drawn from {{n:standards}} instruments and {{n:provisions}} provisions, grouped into {{n:questions}} questions. "Touches a provision" never means "complies".

**Review.** A third party's card is published only after two rounds of review and a sign-off. [The workflow](../spectrum/pipeline/index.md).

**Trust.** Each fact behind a card is scored by [FACT-Ray](../fact-ray/index.md): its source, its record and who stands behind it, always shown with its coordinates. Trust sits beside a level and never changes it. Every card is a Draft until its company confirms it on record or a customer goes on record. A company can state its own claims in a file at `/.well-known/fact-ray.json` on its own domain.

## What a card does not say

- It does not say a company is good or bad.
- It does not say "no" where nothing was found. It says "not yet evidenced".
- It does not use what a company has not published.
- It is a beta: the rules may change, and a card is re-scored when they do. [What changed so far](../spectrum/findings/index.md).

## Correct a card

Send a public link on [the corrections page](../feedback/index.md). A correction is made in place and dated.
