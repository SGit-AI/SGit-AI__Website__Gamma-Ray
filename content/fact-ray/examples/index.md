# Worked examples

<p class="tag">FACT-Ray {{gen:fr-version}} · built inside GAMMA-Ray first; it will move to its own site</p>

Each example is scored by the same scorer that scores the cards, at every build. Change a table and these change with it.

{{gen:fr-examples}}

## What these show

- A subject's word on its own page (S2 · R4) reads High once a human has checked it, and is still contested when a customer on the record says otherwise. Both claims stay on the page.
- For an operation fact, a claim below R4 is shown and does not satisfy the fact.
- An anonymous source is recorded as S3 at R2, attributed to the newsroom.
- The same authority reads Strong when a panel of independent signers stands behind it, and Low when nobody but its author has read it.
- "No data" has no band. It is not a no.

A real article from newsroom.sgit.ai, scored sentence by sentence, is the next example and is not done yet.
