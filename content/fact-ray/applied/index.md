# Where it is applied

<p class="tag">FACT-Ray {{gen:fr-version}} · built inside GAMMA-Ray first; it will move to its own site</p>

FACT-Ray is applied first to this site's own cards. Each fact behind a card is a node with a claim; the card shows the distribution and never an average. The nodes: [nodes.json](@@/data/fact-ray/nodes.json); each evidence record in [evidence.json](@@/data/evidence.json) carries its coordinates.

{{gen:fr-applied}}

## The status of a card follows trust

- **Draft:** found in public pages and reviewed by agents. Every card here today.
- **Confirmed:** the company has confirmed or corrected it, on record.
- **Attested:** at least one customer has gone on record.

Trust is shown beside maturity and never mixed into it. It never lowers a level: it says how firmly the level stands.

## Not applied yet

The newsrooms and PrompTugal.news do not carry FACT-Ray scores yet.
