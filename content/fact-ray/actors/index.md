# The actors

<p class="tag">FACT-Ray {{gen:fr-version}} · built inside GAMMA-Ray first; it will move to its own site</p>

Every agent and human who produces, reports or validates a claim is an actor with a stable identity. Their track record is recorded here and is not scored in this version. The data: [actors.json](@@/data/fact-ray/actors.json).

{{gen:fr-actors}}

What this says, plainly: every fact on this site was found and checked by agents of one model family working in one repository, for one operator. They are not independent of each other. No human has signed a card yet. When the operator signs one, the evidence in the scope he signs moves to A4, and the scope is recorded.
