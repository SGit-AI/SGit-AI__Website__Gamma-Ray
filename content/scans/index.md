# Scans

A scan answers the questions for one agent, computes its levels, and shows its grant where one has been measured. These are Public tier scans: from public pages only.

## Two kinds of scan

**Our own deployments.** We start with our own agents, because a standard should be tried on its authors first. Both reach the top freedom level and are held two levels lower.

**Products, as examples.** A product is not a deployment: it has no owner, no policy and no mandate until somebody runs it. So a product scan is a starting point, not a verdict on its vendor. It says what the product does and controls as shipped, what it can when its options are on, and what its permissions let it do. Each is built only from the vendor's own documentation and published profiles, has not been seen by the vendor, and will be corrected on request.

## What the examples show

- The same product sits at different levels in different shapes. Claude Code on a developer's own machine asks before it acts and has no boundary by default; the same agent in its cloud shape acts on its own inside a container with a proxy above it.
- Freedom is mostly a property of the product. Governance is mostly a property of the deployment: who owns it, what policy it runs under, whether anybody watches.
- A measured grant changes the reading. Where one exists, it shows how many capabilities sit behind a boundary, which no description can.

## Every scan is a vault
