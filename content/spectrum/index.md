# What an agent can do, and how openly it shows it.

<p class="lede">A Spectrum card describes one agentic product by its shape: four dimensions, four levels each, read only from what is public. Every level is something a company can point at. Nothing on a card is a judgement: each level is computed from evidence by a published rule.</p>

{{gen:spectrum-cards}}

These first {{n:cards}} are our own two agents and three widely used products. A standard is tried on its authors first.

## Four dimensions, four levels

Two dimensions measure agentic **power** and two measure agentic **trust**. No dimension caps another. A dimension with no evidence reads "not yet evidenced", never zero.

{{gen:spectrum-dimensions}}

Levels three and four need artefacts, not adjectives: a page, an address that resolves, a case study that names a customer, a report. Marketing, roadmaps and demonstrations are "announced": shown, and never counted.

## The layers

The public card is the front door. Everything that needs the operator sits above it, behind an invitation.

| Layer | Who sees it | Made from | Gives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Spectrum card | public | public evidence only | signature, stage, badges, constellation |
| 2. Claimed card | public, marked as claims | the company adds links and answers the Declared questions | higher levels where evidence supports them; the Claimed badge |
| 3. [GAMMA-Ray rating](../rating/index.md) | the company; published with consent | Declared and Evidenced tiers, operation facts observed | freedom, governance, the level held, a work order |
| 4. RiskMandate | the company only | the measured grant, the mandate, the barriers | the gap, and what would close it |

[RiskMandate](../lens/index.md) is not in the public layer: mandate, grant and delta need the operator. Five of the questions (the registry, accountability, policy, incidents, readiness) feed no public level; a company answers them when it claims its card.

## The rules

1. **Say what is there.** A card lists evidence found. Absence appears only as a next unlock.
2. **Not evidenced is not no.**
3. **A card needs something to show.** No card is published unless one dimension reaches level one. Anyone else gets an invitation.
4. **Published as beta, then the company is told.** A card is published once it has had two rounds of review and a sign-off. The company is told afterwards and invited to [correct or claim it](../feedback/index.md) and add evidence.
5. **No adjectives, no ranking by number.** Cohorts sort by name; a leader is named per dimension, never overall.
6. **Announced is shown, not scored.**
7. **Corrections are dated and propagate.**
8. **The same rules for us.** Our own agents get cards first, as participant entries.

Every earlier rule of GAMMA-Ray stays: levels are computed, links are signed, a scan only reads, and everything carries the beta mark.
