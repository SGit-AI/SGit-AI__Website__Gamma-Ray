# GAMMA-Ray Spectrum — a positive, evidence-backed agentic maturity model

Nov 10, 2026 · @Dinis Cruz

## TL;DR

GAMMA-Ray currently opens with governance, so every card leads with what a company is "held at" and what it lacks. The trick we're missing: flip the order. Lead with a public, positive measure of **agentic power and maturity**, and let the 21-question standards mapping sit underneath as the evidence engine that proves it.

- **Four dimensions, four levels each:** Autonomy, Reach, Control, Openness. Every level is something a company can point at and be proud of.
- **No RiskMandate in the public layer.** Mandate, grant and delta need the operator, so they become an invited, private "level up" tier, not a public score.
- **The standards mapping becomes a reward:** each piece of public evidence lights up provisions across the 8 instruments, shown as a "standards constellation", not a compliance gap.
- **Cohort spotlights, not league tables:** five players in a sector each get a signature, and each dimension names a cohort leader.
- **Every level is a graph walk:** company → product → evidence (captured, hashed, dated) → indicator → level, and indicator → provision → instrument. Two independent agent assessors, a challenger, a gate, then a human.

First use: a PrompTugal edition on Portuguese (then Brazilian) agentic companies, and a "claim your card" hook for the companies we meet at Web Summit on Thursday 12 November.

## The model: four dimensions, four levels

Two dimensions measure agentic **power** (Autonomy, Reach) and two measure agentic **trust** (Control, Openness). That keeps GAMMA-Ray's freedom/governance split, but every rung is an achievement, and no dimension caps another. A dimension with no evidence shows as "not yet evidenced", never as zero or failure.

| Dimension | Question it answers | L1 | L2 | L3 | L4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Autonomy** | What does the agent do on its own? | **Assists**: answers, drafts, summarises; a person does each step | **Suggests**: uses tools and data, proposes actions, a person approves each one | **Acts**: completes routine tasks end to end in production; a person handles exceptions | **Orchestrates**: runs multi-step workflows across systems or coordinates other agents; a person sets goals and reviews outcomes |
| **Reach** | How connected is it? | **Contained**: works inside its own product | **Integrated**: documented connectors to common tools (email, CRM, ERP, ticketing) | **Open protocol**: a published agent interface (MCP server, A2A agent card or agent API) with documented auth | **Ecosystem**: others build on it: marketplace listings, third-party skills or agents, named integrations by partners |
| **Control** | Can people stay in command? | **Stated**: docs say how people approve, override or stop it | **Configurable**: approval modes, permission scopes and roles are documented settings | **Observable**: audit logs, agent identity, pause or kill switch, monitoring available to customers | **Enforced**: limits enforced outside the agent (sandbox, scoped tokens, policy engine), described in a public security architecture |
| **Openness** | Does it show its work? | **Disclosed**: says it is AI and what it is for | **Documented**: public docs on how it works, data use, models and dependencies, limitations | **Measured**: publishes evals or benchmarks, a changelog, an AI policy or trust centre, machine-readable docs (llms.txt, AGENTS.md) | **Verified**: an independent party has checked it: certification (e.g. ISO/IEC 42001), published audit or red-team summary, open-source components |

**Rules for climbing.** Levels are cumulative within a dimension. L3 and L4 need artefacts, not adjectives: a doc page, an endpoint that resolves, a case study naming a customer, a report. Marketing copy, roadmaps and demos count as **announced** and are shown, but never lift a level.

**How it maps to what we already have.** Each level is a pattern over GAMMA-Ray indicators, so the existing provision links carry over unchanged.

| Dimension | GAMMA-Ray questions | Main instruments behind them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Autonomy | Q02 acts or answers, Q05 person's role | Salesforce, EAAMM, Microsoft |
| Reach | Q01 built on, Q03 reach, Q04 works with other agents | OWASP, MCP Authorization, NIST AI RMF, AAGMM |
| Control | Q07 identity and least access, Q11 step in or stop, Q12 logging, Q13 monitoring, Q14 what stands in the way | OWASP, EU AI Act, AAGMM, Microsoft |
| Openness | Q06 purpose, Q15 testing, Q17 people know it's AI, Q18 data rules, Q19 dependencies, Q20 independent check | NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, OWASP, Salesforce |

Q08–Q10, Q16 and Q21 (registry, accountability, policy, incident handling, organisational readiness) are mostly Declared-tier. They move to the claimed-card tier (see the card section), where companies can add them to climb.

Starting indicator set: GAMMA-Ray's 40 Public-tier indicators, trimmed to about 6 per dimension so a scan stays cheap and repeatable.

Underneath it sit real GAMMA-Ray questions, and each level is reached when particular indicators under those questions have evidence. 14 of the 16 cells can be built entirely from GAMMA-Ray's existing 69 indicators. **Worked chain, the one you asked about (Autonomy L1).** The dimension question is "What does the agent do on its own?". **New indicators to add to GAMMA-Ray** (each needs provision links, signed, like the other 69):

- **S1 In production:** a public source names at least one customer, or gives usage figures, for the autonomous capability. Separates "can act" from "acts for someone". Likely links: Salesforce advancement steps, Microsoft adoption items, NIST MAP-1.4 (value).
- **S2 Others build on it:** third parties publish integrations, skills or agents for it in a marketplace, registry or repository. Likely links: Salesforce L4 / ecosystem items, AAGMM GD-09.
- **S3 Machine-readable for agents:** it publishes llms.txt, AGENTS.md, an OpenAPI spec or an agent card for other agents to read. Optional at O3; probe-checkable. Likely links: MCP Authorization metadata items, OWASP inventory items.

Two things this mapping surfaced. First, Control L3 rests on indicators GAMMA-Ray treats as Declared (I39, I41, I43). From outside we can only see that the feature exists in docs, so the card should mark it "available", as GAMMA-Ray already does for product examples. Second, Q08–Q10 and Q21 (registry, accountability, policy, readiness) don't feed any public level. They stay in the Claimed tier, where they can earn a badge but not raise a level.

## The card: signature, stage, badges, spotlights

The card leads with a four-letter **signature**, not a single number, so a company is described by its shape. GAMMA-Ray's rule "never show one number alone" survives; the negative "held at" does not.

```markdown
ACME Legal AI · "Clause" contract agent            SPECTRUM 0.1 BETA · Public tier · 2026-11-11

A3 · R3 · C2 · O3        Stage: Scaling (11/16)         Badges: Balanced · Open protocol

Standout: published MCP server with OAuth scopes; eval report on 4 contract types
Next unlock: Control L3 — a public page on audit logs would get there
Standards constellation: 41 provisions lit across 7 of 8 instruments
Evidence: 14 sources, captured and hashed · vault read key on the card page
Not reviewed by ACME yet · claim this card to add evidence
```

**Headline stage** (sum of the four levels, 0–16):

| Stage | Sum | Reads as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exploring | 1–5 | Agentic features are live and documented |
| Building | 6–9 | Agents do real work, with controls taking shape |
| Scaling | 10–13 | Agents act in production, connect openly, show their work |
| Pioneering | 14–16 | Sets the bar others in the sector measure against |

Every company that qualifies for a card starts at Exploring. There is no stage below it on a published card.

**Badges** are additive rewards. None is taken away for missing something.

- **Balanced:** Control and Openness are each at least Autonomy minus one. This is GAMMA-Ray's held-level rule turned into a reward: power matched by trust earns a mark instead of power beyond trust earning a penalty.
- **Open protocol:** Reach L3 via a published, resolvable agent endpoint.
- **Shows its work:** Openness L3 or higher with published evals.
- **Verified:** any L4 evidence from an independent party.
- **Rising:** moved up at least one level since the previous edition.
- **Claimed:** the company reviewed the card and added evidence.

**Cohort spotlights.** In a sector cohort (say five Portuguese legal-tech or fintech players), each dimension names a cohort leader, and every company gets a "standout" line drawn from its strongest evidence. Readers see who is ahead, and every company still has something worth sharing.

**Three tiers, carried from GAMMA-Ray:** Public (we read it from outside), Claimed (the company added links and answered the short questionnaire; shown as claims), Evidenced (shared records or a live check; invited only). The tier is printed on the card.

## Evidence, provenance and the semantic graph

Every level on a card is a walk through a graph that anyone can open: click A3 and you land on the evidence, the indicator it satisfies, the rule that computed the level, and the provisions that indicator touches. This reuses the GAMMA-Ray mashup and the sgit fractal-graph grammar; we add the company, product and evidence layers on top.

| Node | Holds | Opens into |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company | Name, country, sector, cohort | Its products |
| Product (agent) | One agentic product, as documented | Its card for each edition |
| Card | Signature, stage, badges, tier, edition date, Spectrum version | Its levels |
| Level | Dimension, level, rule that fired | The indicators that met the rule |
| Indicator | One observable fact (GAMMA-Ray I01–I69) | Evidence and provisions |
| Evidence | URL, captured copy, SHA-256, read date, quoted span (under 15 words), method (read / probe / claimed) | The source capture in the vault |
| Provision | One clause of one instrument | Its instrument |
| Assertion | Who linked what, when, with a written basis and a strength | Nothing; it signs an edge |

Edges: company *offers* product; product *has* card; card *rated* level; level *met-by* indicator; indicator *evidenced-by* evidence; indicator *touches* provision (same / part / absence); every edge *signed-by* assertion.

An evidence record, as the agents write it:

```json
{
  "id": "ev:acme/clause/2026-11-11/007",
  "indicator": "I10",
  "url": "https://docs.example.com/mcp",
  "captured": "vault:spectrum-pt-ed1/captures/007.html",
  "sha256": "…",
  "read_at": "2026-11-11T09:14Z",
  "method": "probe",
  "span": "MCP server with OAuth 2.1 scopes per workspace",
  "assessor": ["assessor-a@spectrum", "assessor-b@spectrum"],
  "agreement": "both",
  "challenger": "pass"
}
```

Three properties make it trustworthy rather than just tidy:

1. **Same evidence, same level.** Levels come from published rules over indicators, never from an assessor's judgement of the level itself (GAMMA-Ray rule 3).
2. **Captured, not just linked.** Pages change; the hashed capture is what the level was computed from, as pt.newsroom already does.
3. **Machine-checkable where possible.** Many Reach and Openness indicators are probes, not readings: does `/.well-known/agent-card.json` resolve, is there an `llms.txt`, is the MCP server listed in a registry, does the OAuth metadata endpoint answer. Probes are read-only and send no credentials (GAMMA-Ray rule 5).

Outputs per edition: one vault per card (read key on the card page), plus `cards.json`, `evidence.json` and JSON-LD on each card page so other agents can consume it.

## Standards constellation: the mapping as a reward

The 21-question mapping is the most valuable asset we have, and today it mostly shows what's missing. Turned around, it shows the power of the standards: one public doc page can light up provisions in five frameworks at once. GAMMA-Ray's own mashup already found that two indicators each touch six instruments, and 28 of 69 indicators are asked for by three or more.

On each card, the constellation shows, per instrument, how many provisions the company's public evidence touches:

| Instrument | Provisions | Public-readable share (from [coverage](https://gamma-ray.sgit.ai/coverage/)) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salesforce Agentic Maturity Model | 34 | 65% |
| EAAMM (Emelyanov, 2026) | 12 | 67% |
| OWASP State of Agentic AI Security and Governance 2.01 | 35 | 49% |
| MCP Authorization (draft) | 15 | 53% |
| AAGMM (Acharya, 2026) | 12 | 50% |
| Microsoft agentic AI adoption maturity model | 88 | 25% |
| NIST AI RMF 1.0 | 72 | 26% |
| EU AI Act, selected articles | 11 | 55% |

Two framing rules keep it honest. "Touched" never means "complies" (the mashup page says the same of "reached"). And the denominator shown is the public-readable share, so a company is measured against what can be seen from outside, not against all 279 provisions.

The pitch to companies writes itself: "Your public docs already speak to 41 provisions across seven frameworks. Here's which page did it."

Two additions worth making: ISO/IEC 42001 and 27001, which RiskMandate already cites but GAMMA-Ray hasn't read at source, and a Portuguese / Brazilian lens (Portugal's AI agenda and the AI Act as applied; Brazil's AI bill and LGPD) for the PrompTugal editions.

## Where GAMMA-Ray governance and RiskMandate fit

They become the upper floors, not the front door. The public Spectrum card is read from outside; everything that needs the operator moves behind an invitation.

| Layer | Who sees it | Inputs | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Spectrum card | Public | Public evidence only | Signature, stage, badges, constellation |
| 2. Claimed card | Public, marked as claims | Company adds links and answers the Declared questions (registry, owner, policy, incidents, readiness) | Higher levels where evidence supports; Claimed badge |
| 3. GAMMA-Ray deployment rating | Company, publishable by consent | Declared and Evidenced tiers, operation facts observed | Freedom, governance, held level, work order |
| 4. RiskMandate ABP | Company only | Measured grant, elicited mandate, barriers | Gap, enforcement level, licence to operate |

The funnel is the business model: a public card is free and flattering, claiming it is a 10-minute action, and the gap a company sees between layers 2 and 3 is exactly what a RiskMandate engagement closes. The negative language is still there, but only where a company has asked for it.

## Positivity and fairness rules

Positive does not mean soft: the levels stay strict, and the language says what is there. These rules extend GAMMA-Ray's seven conduct rules.

1. **Say what is there.** A card lists evidence found. Absence appears only as a "next unlock", phrased as the one artefact that would move a level.
2. **Not evidenced is not no.** Kept from GAMMA-Ray; a blank dimension reads "not yet evidenced".
3. **Only publish cards with something to show.** A company enters a cohort only if at least one dimension reaches L1. Anyone else gets an invitation, not a card.
4. **Right of reply before publication.** The company receives its draft card with 72 hours to add evidence. Most replies raise a level, which is the point.
5. **No adjectives, no rankings by number.** Cohort views sort by name; leaders are named per dimension, never as an overall winner.
6. **Announced is shown, not scored.** Roadmap and demo claims appear in grey so companies get credit for direction without it inflating levels.
7. **Corrections propagate and are dated.** A correction updates the card, the cohort view and the PrompTugal piece, with a changelog entry (as newsroom.sgit.ai already does).
8. **Same rules for us.** Our own agents (RiskMandate Publisher, the PrompTugal team) get Spectrum cards first and appear in the first edition as "participant" entries.

## The agentic pipeline that runs it

The same conductor pattern as web-summit.sgit.ai, with two changes that fix GAMMA-Ray's "one reader, never checked" weakness: two assessors work blind to each other, and a challenger tries to knock each level down before the gate.

1. **Scout** builds the cohort from the pt.newsroom graph, the Web Summit company map and Startup Portugal / Brazilian ecosystem lists; picks one flagship agentic product per company.
2. **Collector** finds candidate sources (docs, trust centre, changelog, case studies, registries), captures and hashes them into the edition vault, and runs read-only probes.
3. **Assessor A and Assessor B** (different models or prompts, no shared context) each map evidence to indicators. They never assign levels.
4. **Scorer** (deterministic code, not an agent) computes levels, stage, badges and constellation from the agreed indicators.
5. **Challenger** argues against every L3 and L4 and every disagreement between A and B; unresolved items go to a person.
6. **Gate** checks the rules mechanically: every level has captured evidence, no adjectives, quotes under 15 words, tier printed, "not reviewed" line present.
7. **Editor** writes the PrompTugal piece and the per-company standout lines, in Portuguese and English.
8. **Ambassador** drafts the right-of-reply email and the "claim your card" follow-up; a person sends it.

What a person still does: approve the cohort, resolve challenger escalations, sign off each edition, and send outreach. Everything else runs on the two-hour conductor cadence.

## Risks and open questions

- **Gaming.** Companies will write pages to climb. That is mostly a win (more public evidence), and L3/L4 need artefacts that are expensive to fake: resolvable endpoints, named customers, third-party reports.
- **Size bias.** Big vendors document more. Cohorts by sector and size, and probes that a two-person startup passes as easily as a corporate, keep it fair.
- **Product vs. deployment.** A card rates a product as documented, not anyone's deployment of it. Say so on every card, as GAMMA-Ray already does with "deployment governance: not assessed".
- **Legal.** Public data only, no adjectives, right of reply and dated corrections; still worth a quick read by a Portuguese lawyer before the first edition.
- **Name.** "GAMMA-Ray Spectrum" keeps the brand and the light metaphor (bands, not a score). Alternatives: "Agentic Spectrum", "PrompTugal Agentic Index". Your call.
- **Open:** sum-based stage or a stage named from the signature's shape? Should Balanced be a badge or part of the stage? Which first sector cohort: fintech, legal, health, or customer service?
